Who we are

Bailey & Johnston are an independent family-owned practice of Chartered Surveyors who specialise in residential surveying.

Our surveyors are experienced Chartered Building Surveyors and Registered Valuers who have gained extensive experience across the residential sector which enables us to provide expert advice to our clients.

As members of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (MRICS), our surveyors have the appropriate level of expert knowledge and work to the highest professional and ethical standards.

We are passionate about our work and providing excellent customer service to our clients. Why do you need a Home Survey?

Whether you're buying a modern property or a run-down period property, a Home Survey will enable you to be fully aware of what you are intending to purchase, will help you minimise your financial risk and ensure you avoid any unpleasant surprises when you move into your new home.

Our surveys will advise you how the property is built and will identify defects such as damp, structural issues, non-compliant building alterations among many others which could easily be missed during a viewing.

Meet the Team

Lee Johnston BSc (Hons) MRICS - Director

Lee is a Chartered Building Surveyor with over 10 years of experience, specialising primarily in the residential sector.

He graduated with first-class honours in Building Surveying from Liverpool John Moores University and later became a professional member of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (MRICS), achieving Chartered status.

Lee is passionate about residential surveying and takes pride in helping clients solve complex property related problems. He currently specialises in the following areas:

◦ Delivering comprehensive RICS Level 2 and Level 3 Home Survey reports to support clients during the homebuying process

◦ Advising clients on the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 in relation to construction works near shared boundaries

◦ Providing accurate RICS Valuation reports for a variety of purposes

◦ Producing Expert Witness Housing Disrepair reports, predominantly on behalf of solicitors acting for housing associations (Defendants)

Lee is also a member of the Pyramus and Thisbe Society, a professional organisation dedicated to promoting best practice and knowledge surrounding the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 and construction near boundaries.
