Starting Construction Work on the Wirral? Know Your Responsibilities

You may be legally required to notify neighbours under the Party Wall etc. Act if your works include:

  • Building close to or astride a boundary line
  • Making alterations to a wall shared with another property
  • Excavating foundations within proximity of a neighbouring building
  • Demolishing or rebuilding any shared boundary feature

Our surveyors will handle the process from notice to resolution, keeping everything compliant and helping prevent disputes before they start.

Have You Been Given a Party Wall Notice?

If you’ve received a notice about upcoming works next door, you have a set of legal rights, including:

  • Appointing your own independent Party Wall Surveyor (typically at the expense of the building owner)
  • Requesting a Schedule of Condition to protect your property’s condition
  • Raising concerns about the proposed work before anything starts
  • Securing compensation or remedial work if damage occurs

We regularly represent Adjoining Owners across Wirral, helping ensure that your interests are looked after throughout the process.

What the Party Wall Act Means for You

This legislation provides a clear procedure for property owners to follow when construction affects shared structures or foundations. It’s especially useful in areas like Wirral, where properties often sit close together and renovations are common.

Our role is to make that process easier, ensuring everyone involved understands their rights and obligations, and helping resolve any disagreements fairly.
