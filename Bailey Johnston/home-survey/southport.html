Our Survey Services

RICS Level 2 Home Survey (HomeBuyer Report)

An excellent choice for traditional-style homes in reasonable condition. This intermediate-level inspection highlights major structural concerns, defects, and urgent maintenance issues. A clear, concise format makes it accessible and practical for most property buyers.

Ideal for:

  • Standard housing stock in satisfactory condition
  • Getting clarity on your property’s condition quickly

RICS Level 3 Home Survey (Building Survey)

Bailey & Johnston’s most thorough residential inspection. Best for older, altered, or architecturally unusual homes, this survey dives deep into structural integrity, construction fabric, and potential hidden issues.

Perfect for:

  • Period properties or homes with extensive modifications
  • Properties requiring detailed insight before purchase
Survey Type Best For Key Benefits
Level 2 HomeBuyer Report Traditional homes in reasonable condition Cost-effective insight into defects, maintenance, and safety
Level 3 Building Survey Older, altered, or architecturally unique properties In-depth structural evaluation & detailed construction analysis
