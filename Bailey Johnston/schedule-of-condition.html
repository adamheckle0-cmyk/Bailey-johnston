Schedule of Condition

What is a Schedule of Condition?

A Schedule of Condition is a report which details the condition of a residential or commercial property at a specific point in time and is usually prepared for contractual or legal reasons. The report will detail the full condition of the property externally and internally which can then be used as a benchmark to assess the condition of the property at a later date.

When is a Schedule of Condition suitable?

  • If you are a Landlord or Tenant and you want to document the condition of the property prior to entering into a lease. This can help to avoid potential disputes and claims for damages during or at the end of a lease.
  • If you are a property owner and you would like a report which documents the condition of your property prior to any extensive works being undertaken to your property or a neighbouring property. This can be used as evidence should any damage occur to your property. This can help to avoid disputes during works which are notifiable under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996.
  • If you are a property owner with a portfolio of properties and you would like a better understanding of the condition of your assets. This can assist with budgeting for future repair and maintenance costs.

What is included in a Schedule of Condition?

The purpose of the report

The date and time of the inspection which will be carried out by an RICS Chartered Building Surveyor

A general brief description of the subject property

Limitations of the inspection

Floor plan drawing if appliable

A schedule which details the description of each external and internal building element which has been inspected. This will include a list of defects identified such as cracks, disrepair, damp, leaks and general deterioration

Photographic evidence of each defect
