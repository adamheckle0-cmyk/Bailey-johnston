Valuation Reports

What is a valuation?

An independent valuation report provides a general overview of the property and a current Market Valuation in accordance with RICS requirements.

It is important to note that a valuation is not a survey and if you are purchasing a property we would recommend a Level 2 Home Survey as a minimum which can include a Market Valuation if required.

When is a valuation report suitable?

An independent valuation report is suitable if you require a market valuation for any of the following circumstances:

  • Help to Buy Valuation
  • Shared Ownership Valuation
  • Matrimonial / Divorce settlement Valuation
  • If you are purchasing or selling a property and require an accurate valuation
  • Probate/Inheritance Tax Valuation

What is included in a valuation?

  • An external and internal inspection of the property
  • A general description of the property, accommodation, and floor area
  • A current market valuation of the property
  • A reinstatement cost of the property for insurance purposes (if applicable)
  • Comparable evidence of recent property sales

Building defects are only assessed if they are likely to have a detrimental effect on the value of the property. Independent valuation reports do not include any description or advice on any building defects.
